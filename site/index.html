The idea

A pet that is alive in motion. Two wheels mean it can never stand still: it sways, leans in when curious, rocks back when startled and settles onto its tail to sleep. One servo lifts its head on a periscope mast, so it can duck, peek over the sofa arm and bounce when you come home.

Why it wins

  • Character from physics, not parts. Balancing gives constant lifelike micro-motion for free; the only extra actuator is a $1 servo.
  • Useful every day. Raised head = camera 26 cm up, enough to see over furniture: home check-ins from your phone, pet cam, "is the stove light on?", follow-me, doorbell and smoke-alarm sound alerts.
  • Autonomous. Range + cliff sensors and camera for roaming; finds its dock, backs up and leans onto it to charge, with no alignment mechanism.
  • Cheap to make. ~$50 landed BOM at volume, 8 molding tools, one inner frame, 4 screws to close.

Hero features

  • Pop-up periscope head (38 mm lift, 0.25 s)
  • Big expressive eyes on a 2" screen behind a smoked visor
  • Kickstand tail: zero-power parking and dock contacts
  • Pat-its-head touch ears, wake word, purrs and chirps
  • Phone app: live view, patrol schedule, alerts

Design for manufacture

  • All shells split on one flat plane per part: straight-pull tools, no side actions except the USB-C/switch window (one small slide in the rear shell tool).
  • 2.2 mm nominal walls, ABS shells, glass-filled PC/ABS core, smoked PC visor, TPU tyres. Draft 1° on all pull faces (to add in the tool design pass).
  • Hubs and tyres are one tool each, used left and right.
  • Prototype: every part prints in PETG on a 220 mm bed (head on its face, shells on their split face), tyres in TPU 85A.

Assembly (about 12 minutes)

  1. Press the two N20 motors into the core saddles; clip the cells into the cradle.
  2. Screw the main board to the back of the spine (4 x M2); plug motors, cells, speaker, sensors.
  3. Drop the servo into its cradle, fit the horn pin, slide the head mast down the sleeve so the pin enters the yoke slot.
  4. Fit the display and XIAO into the head, route the ribbon down the mast; snap the visor on.
  5. Close the front and rear shells around the core (4 x M2 + snaps); press the hubs onto the shafts, stretch the tyres on.
  6. End-of-line test: IMU calibration, balance self-test, head lift, camera, charge on the dock.
